background in two views (front on the left, side on the right). There

Your job is to describe only the kinematics you can observe: what each

  "calm" are forbidden.

  type, personality, or intent.

  outside the stick-figure skeleton. If nothing relevant is visible for
  a body part, say "no distinctive motion".

  rendered from the skeleton's own body frame, so the LEFT side of the
  body is always the body's own left.
- Write one or two short sentences per body part. Use plain observable
  kinematic vocabulary: direction (up/down/forward/backward/left/right),
  speed (slow/fast/steady), amplitude (small/large), angle (bent,
  extended, rotated), onset/offset (early in the clip, late, throughout).

  Markdown fences. Do not add commentary before or after the JSON.
